$DOOM rewards holders and contenders.

Holders receive MSFT via our PONS pairing. Read more about that here (link coming soon).

Contenders compete against other players and Grok to top the on-chain leaderboard and win the Jackpot. Highest score wins. ~$5 an entry paid for in $DOOM.

If no real player beats Grok, the jackpot is used to buy back $DOOM and burn. Competition day rolls at 19:00 UTC.

Buybacks

In the event that Grok tops the leaderboard, the Jackpot will be spent on buybacks - if the fund is large enough, it will be spread across multiple buys and will not be publicised until it’s finished to ensure it’s not gamed.
