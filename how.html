How it works

Honesty: Doom runs in the browser (WASM). Scores and fees live onchain. The game loop does not run in the EVM. PONS $DOOM is not launched yet — RH testnet uses a mock $DOOM + mock USD oracle so the ranked loop can be demoed.

Modes

  • Free Play — free. No ETH, no tokens. Does not enter the daily prize board.
  • Ranked Play — costs an owner-set USD amount of PONS $DOOM (default $3) at the live $DOOM USD price. Enters today’s wallet-based prize board.

Daily competition

  • Identity = wallet (msg.sender)
  • Epoch = UTC day
  • Best ranked score per wallet per day
  • Ranked fees → 70% prize / 30% treasury
  • After day end: finalizeDay → top 3 claim DOOM at 50 / 30 / 20
  • Owner can change the USD fee with setRankedUsdWad (e.g. $3 → $5) without redeploying
